## Key findings during the investigation

- The AIS-57 gate is **satisfied by evidence**, not by status: AIS-58 … AIS-63 are all `Done`,
  while the AIS-57 story itself is still `To Do`. Flagged on the ticket for the reporter; not
  changed here.
- The page can be built almost entirely from what already exists: `apiKeysService`,
  `useApiKeysQuery` / `useCreateApiKeyMutation`, `toIdentifierFieldError`, `toExpiryIso` /
  `formatExpiry`, `toErrorMessage`, and the six `@callback/ui` primitives.
- Three real gaps had to become decisions instead of assumptions:
  1. `react-hook-form`, `@hookform/resolvers` and a **direct** `zod` are not installed in
     `apps/admin` (D29, D30) — and zod must stay on v3 to match `packages/shared`.
  2. `packages/ui` has **no multi-line input**, while `description` allows 2000 characters
     (D40 — a `Textarea` primitive is added to the kit instead of an inline one-off).
  3. An `<input type="date">` value never satisfies the shared `createApiKeySchema` (ISO
     instant), so the form needs its own schema plus a pure mapping function (D31, D32).
- `apiKey` must never reach the DOM: the view model simply has no such field (D33), asserted by
  two tests (mapper and table).
