## Notes and consequences

* **Shared files, ownership over build order.** AIS-92 and AIS-93 interleave in
  `apps/api/src/main.ts` and `apps/api/test/api-keys.e2e-spec.ts`; AIS-95 and AIS-96
  interleave in the admin `api-keys` feature/page constants, interfaces, the query hook test
  and the `api-error` mocks. Those files are committed with the ticket that owns the majority
  of their diff, and the commit bodies record the sibling lines they carry.
* **Intermediate commits are not individually buildable.** `AIS-95` references constants that
  `AIS-96` finalises, so only `AIS-96` compiles in isolation — the same trade-off the AIS-87
  split documents. Only `HEAD` is guaranteed green.
* `AIS-92` also commits the `database-gate` e2e helper, which lets the persistence suite
  self-skip loudly instead of failing with `ECONNREFUSED` on a machine without Postgres.
* `.github/git-commit-instructions.md` (empty, untracked, no ticket to attribute it to) was
  deliberately left out of the split.
